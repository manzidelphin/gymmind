How GymMind collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Rwanda Data Protection Law No. 058/2021 Compliant

Who We Are

GymMind is operated by Dellon Technologies Ltd, registered in Rwanda. We provide gym management software to gyms and fitness centers across Rwanda and East Africa.

Data Controller: The gym you are a member of is the primary data controller. GymMind acts as a data processor on behalf of gyms.

We do not collect health information, biometrics, or financial account numbers.

How We Use Your Data

  • Authentication via OTP-based login — no passwords stored
  • Check-in tracking for attendance and billing
  • Generating invoices for individual and institutional memberships
  • SMS reminders for expiring memberships
  • Providing attendance reports to gym owners and HR managers

Data Sharing

We share your data only with: your gym manager/owner, your employer's HR administrator (if institutional), MTN MoMo (payment verification), and Africa's Talking (SMS delivery). We never sell personal data to third parties.

Security

  • Multi-tenant architecture — each gym's data is fully isolated
