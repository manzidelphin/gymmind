Data Processing Agreement

This DPA governs how GymMind processes personal data on behalf of gyms and institutions.

⚖ Rwanda Data Protection Law No. 058/2021 Compliant

Parties & Definitions

Data Controller — The gym or institution that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.

Data Processor — GymMind (Dellon Technologies Ltd), which processes personal data on behalf of the Controller.

Personal Data — Any information relating to an identified or identifiable person (name, phone number, attendance records).

This DPA forms part of the agreement between GymMind and its customers and is incorporated into the Terms of Service.

GymMind Obligations (Processor)

  • Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Data Controller
  • Ensure all staff with data access are bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
  • Not engage sub-processors without prior Controller authorization
  • Assist the Controller in responding to data subject rights requests
  • Delete or return all personal data upon termination of services
  • Notify the Controller of any data breach within 72 hours

Technical Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
  • Access Control: Role-based access — gyms only see their own data
  • Authentication: OTP-based, no stored passwords
  • Audit Logging: All system access and changes are logged
  • Backup: Daily automated backups with 30-day retention
  • Monitoring: 24/7 system monitoring and intrusion detection

Data Breach Notification

  • GymMind will notify the Data Controller within 72 hours of discovering a breach
  • Notification includes: nature of breach, data categories affected, individuals affected, and remediation measures
  • The Controller is responsible for notifying data subjects and the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) of Rwanda where required

Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the laws of the Republic of Rwanda, including Law No. 058/2021 on Data Protection and Privacy. Disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Kigali, Rwanda.
