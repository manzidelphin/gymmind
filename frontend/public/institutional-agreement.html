Institutional Agreement

Corporate gym access agreement for companies and institutions partnering with GymMind gyms.

For: Companies · Government Agencies · NGOs · Universities

Agreement Overview

This Agreement governs the corporate gym access program between an Institution and a Partner Gym through the GymMind platform.

The Institution

The company or organization enrolling its employees for gym access (e.g., RwandAir, Bank of Kigali, MTN Rwanda, RDB, RRA)

The Partner Gym

The gym facility providing physical access to enrolled employees and their eligible dependents

GymMind facilitates this relationship by managing check-ins, billing, and reporting through its platform.

Employee Enrollment

  • The Institution provides a list of eligible employees in Excel format (Name, Phone, Parent Name columns)
  • Each employee claims their account using their phone number and OTP verification
  • Only claimed (active) accounts can check into the gym
  • The Institution is responsible for notifying employees of their gym access benefit
  • Employees can be added or removed at any time through the HR Portal
Employee data is only accessible to authorized HR administrators and the partner gym's managers. Data is never shared with other institutions.

Dependent Access

  • Institutions may choose to allow dependents (family members) of employees
  • Dependents are linked to their primary employee in the system via the "Parent Name" column
  • Dependent access can be enabled or disabled per institution
  • Dependent visits are billed at the same rate as employee visits

Check-in Process

  • Employees check in using their registered phone number
  • A 6-digit OTP is sent via SMS to verify identity at the gym desk
  • Each check-in is recorded with timestamp, gym location, and service used
  • The same service cannot be used twice within 10 hours (anti-abuse protection)
  • Different services (gym, boxing, sauna, swimming pool) can be used on the same day

  • Invoices generated automatically at month end
  • Invoice includes per-employee breakdown with visit dates and services
  • PDF invoices downloadable from the HR Portal
  • Payment via MTN MoMo or bank transfer
  • Payment due within 30 days of invoice date
  • Late payments may result in temporary suspension of gym access

HR Portal Features

Each institution receives a dedicated HR Portal where administrators can:

  • View real-time attendance for all enrolled employees
  • Add or remove employees individually or in bulk via Excel
  • Download PDF invoices and billing reports
  • Pay invoices directly via MTN MoMo
  • View monthly cost breakdowns per employee

Institution Obligations

  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date employee lists
  • Notify GymMind when employees leave the company
  • Pay invoices on time
  • Ensure employees have consented to data sharing with the gym
  • Not enroll non-employees or unauthorized individuals
  • Designate at least one authorized HR administrator for the portal

Termination

  • Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice
  • Outstanding invoices must be settled upon termination
  • Employee accounts deactivated upon termination
  • Historical attendance data available for 90 days after termination
